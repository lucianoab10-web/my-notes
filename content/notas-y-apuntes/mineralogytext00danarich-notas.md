> Crystallographic Axes. The orthorhombic system includes all the forms which are referred to three axes at right  angles to each other, all of different lengths. Any one of the three axes may be taken as the vertical axis, c. Of the two horizontal axes the longer is always taken as the b or macro-axis * and when orientated is parallel to the observer. The a or brachy-axis is the shorter of the two horizontal axes and is perpendicular to the observer.
