> Muscovite – rare-element class - p. 2010

> pegmatites of the muscovite – rare-element class contain both high-quality muscovite of economic potential (e.g., ruby grade), and concentrations of rare-element minerals that in rare cases verge on economic (e.g., beryl, cassiterite, columbite-group minerals, REE–Nb–U oxides, Li silicates). - p. 2010
